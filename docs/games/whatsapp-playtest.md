# Step 0 — WhatsApp playtest kit (no code)

Run **Who Knows Us Best?** by hand in an existing WhatsApp group for **5–7 days**.
You play Chef. The goal: find out whether people *want* to play before we build anything.

## How to run a round

1. Post the question (copy the template below), naming the subject.
2. The **subject** sends their answer to you **privately** (DM).
3. Everyone else replies in the group with a **letter only** (A/B/C/D).
   Their guesses are visible — that's fine for a paper test.
4. When everyone has guessed, or at 9 pm, post the reveal.
5. Rotate the subject each round. 1–3 rounds per day.

### Question template
```
🍽️ Kitchen Table — Round 3
Who knows MOM best?

Which snack would Mom take on a road trip?
A) Chips  B) Fruit  C) Murukku  D) Chocolate

Mom: DM me your answer 🤫
Everyone else: reply with a letter!
Reveal at 9 pm or when everyone's in.
```

### Reveal template
```
🥁 Reveal — Round 3
Mom picked: C) Murukku

✅ Dad, Arjun
❌ Priya (D)

2 of 3 know Mom's road-trip snack!
🏆 This week: Dad 4 · Arjun 3 · Priya 2
```

## 10 starter questions

1. Which snack would I take on a road trip? — Chips / Fruit / Murukku / Chocolate
2. What's my ideal Sunday morning? — Sleep in / Long walk / Big breakfast / Phone in bed
3. Which superpower would I pick? — Flying / Invisibility / Teleporting / Reading minds
4. What would I order at a new restaurant? — The usual favorite / Chef's special / Whatever's cheapest / Ask the waiter
5. Beach, mountains, city or countryside for a holiday? — Beach / Mountains / City / Countryside
6. Which chore would I happily do forever? — Cooking / Laundry / Dishes / Grocery shopping
7. What do I do first when I wake up? — Check phone / Tea or coffee / Stretch / Snooze again
8. Which movie genre would I pick on a lazy night? — Comedy / Action / Romance / Thriller
9. If I won a small prize, I'd spend it on…? — Food / Clothes / A trip / Save it
10. Which would I rather give up for a month? — Sweets / TV / Social media / Coffee or tea

## What to watch (write it down each day)

| Signal | Good sign | Bad sign |
|---|---|---|
| Participation | Most people answer each round | Same 1–2 people every time |
| Speed | Guesses within a few hours | You have to chase people |
| Replay | Someone asks "next one?" | Silence after the reveal |
| Talk | Jokes and arguments after the reveal | Nobody reacts |
| Confusion | People get it without explanation | Questions about the rules |
| Ideas | People suggest questions | — |

## Decide after the week

- **It worked** (most people played most days, someone asked for more) → build Step 1.
- **Mixed** → change one thing (different questions, 1 round a day, a different group) and run 3 more days.
- **It didn't** → try the Daily Question format the same way before building anything.
